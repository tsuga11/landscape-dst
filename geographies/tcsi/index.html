The Tahoe Central Sierra Initiative (TCSI) Blueprint is a set of strategy maps identifying opportunities for forest protection and adaptation across a 2.4-million-acre region of the central Sierra Nevada. Its aim is to improve resilience to anticipated climate change and wildfire-, beetle-, and drought-caused tree mortality.

This application is powered by the PROMOTE decision support framework (RAD: Resist–Accept–Direct) and uses the MapLibre GL JS mapping engine with Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF data served as PMTiles. Color ramps are applied client-side with GPU shaders — no pre-rendered tile colors.

Blueprint v3 includes five pillars: Forest Resilience, Fire Dynamics, Fire Adapted Communities, Carbon Sequestration, and Biodiversity. Pillar outputs are combined into the Ecosystem-level assessment.

    Monitor — Good condition now and into the future; management intervention may not be necessary in the short term.
    Protect — Currently in good condition but conditions deteriorate over time; treatments may help maintain them.
    Adapt — Currently in poor condition but demonstrate capacity to achieve desired conditions in the future under natural disturbance processes.
    Transform — In poor condition now and remain so over time; these areas may not be first in line for treatment given high uncertainty in management effectiveness.

Impact scores reflect the maximum level of support for either Adapt or Protect strategies — areas where management investment has the highest expected return.
