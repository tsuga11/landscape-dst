Ecosystem Resilience
Cooperative

Research on landscape resilience and biodiversity vulnerability to disturbances and ongoing climate change. Developing spatial decision support tools to explore ecosystem status and change, and prioritize restoration and protection across western US forests and watersheds.

Background

About

The Ecosystem Resilience Cooperative represents a group of ecological researchers whose research is joined by the common goal of evaluating social and ecological resilience across large landscapes using quantitative methods and how climate change may impact resilient properties over time.
